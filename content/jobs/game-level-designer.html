Who we are

AARISE is a young independent studio in La Rochelle, founded by former members of the Tower Five production team. We are the creative and technical people behind Empire of the Ants (released in late 2024, built with Unreal Engine 5). After our previous company closed, our core team of developers chose to come together again and keep creating side by side.

At AARISE, we chose a human, horizontal structure where every voice counts and takes an active part in day-to-day decisions. Building on our Unreal Engine 5 expertise, we are starting production on an ambitious new project: a 3D collectathon platformer. We are looking for our future Game Designer, with a strong Level Design profile, to join us in La Rochelle and co-create the foundations of this new project.

Your missions

Working hand in hand with the development team and the art team, your main missions will be:

  • Gameplay & 3Cs: design, document and balance the core platforming mechanics (Character, Controls, Camera) to deliver precise, fluid and fun game feel.
  • Level design & layouts: design the levels and play spaces as engaging greyboxes. You shape the pacing, the difficulty curve and the way players learn the mechanics.
  • Integration & scripting: implement and fine-tune enemy patterns, obstacles, collectibles and interactive elements directly in the game engine.
  • Art synergy: work daily with the 3D art team to make sure the visuals fully respect gameplay readability and collision constraints.

Your profile

  • Experience: at least 3 years of game and level design on video game productions. Ideally, you have already shipped at least one game (PC or consoles), which gives you a clear view of what the end of a production demands.
  • Technical skills: solid command of a game engine (Unreal Engine and Blueprints are a strong asset for the project); a proven ability to create readable, well-paced level layouts that make the most of verticality; excellent writing skills (clear, actionable GDDs for the team).
  • Team spirit & autonomy: you thrive in horizontal structures. Autonomous and rigorous, you appreciate the absence of micromanagement and know how to make proposals within a team of peers.
  • Gaming culture: a strong taste for, and a deep understanding of, the codes, history and gameplay loops of the platformer genre.

Our recruitment process

We make sure our process is transparent, smooth and focused on dialogue:

  • Step 1: a first 30 to 45-minute video call with our Studio & HR Manager to get to know each other and talk about your expectations.
  • Step 2: a technical test focused on mechanics or level design, followed by a full debrief with the production team to discuss your methodology.
  • Step 3: a final interview with our CEO to confirm your arrival and finalise our collaboration.

What we offer

  • A full-time executive (cadre) position on a 35-hour week, for a real work/life balance.
  • Comprehensive health insurance, 100% paid by the studio.
  • The opportunity to lay the first stones of a new original IP, within a close-knit, experienced team that proved itself on Empire of the Ants.
  • The quality of life of La Rochelle: a lively digital scene, culture, and the ocean right on your doorstep.
