Who we are

AARISE is a young independent studio in La Rochelle, founded by former members of the Tower Five production team. We are the creative and technical people behind Empire of the Ants (released in late 2024, built with Unreal Engine 5). After our previous company closed, our core team of developers chose to come together again and keep creating side by side.

At AARISE, we chose a human, horizontal structure where every voice counts and takes an active part in day-to-day decisions. Building on our Unreal Engine 5 expertise, we are starting production on an ambitious new project: a 3D collectathon platformer. We are looking for our future 3D talent to join us in La Rochelle and help shape the visual identity of this new adventure.

Your missions

Working hand in hand with the art team and the technical team, your main missions will be:

  • Modelling & texturing: design and create 3D assets (sets, environments, props and interactive gameplay elements specific to platforming mechanics).
  • Engine integration: import, set up and optimise your assets directly in the game engine (Unreal Engine).
  • Technical optimisation: ensure visual quality while respecting polygon and texture budgets, to guarantee a perfectly smooth 60 FPS.
  • Gameplay synergy: work daily with the level design team to make sure environments serve the gameplay intentions of the platformer in a fluid, intuitive way.

Your profile

  • Experience: at least 3 years in video game production in a similar role. Ideally, having already shipped a commercial game or a large-scale project is a real plus for this position.
  • Technical skills: advanced command of modelling tools (ideally Blender and/or Maya and/or 3ds Max) and sculpting (ZBrush); excellent command of texturing (Substance Painter / Designer); solid hands-on knowledge of a game engine (Unreal Engine is a strong asset for the project).
  • Team spirit & autonomy: you thrive in horizontal structures. Autonomous and rigorous, you appreciate the absence of micromanagement and know how to make proposals within a team of peers.
  • Artistic sense: a keen sense of composition, scale and light. An affinity for platform games is a plus.

Bonus points (the cherry on top): experience with shading in Unreal, you master Houdini, you are interested in UI, or concept art is your thing.

Our recruitment process

We make sure our process is transparent, smooth and focused on dialogue:

  • Step 1: a first 30 to 45-minute video call with our Studio & HR Manager to get to know each other and talk about your expectations.
  • Step 2: a hands-on technical test, followed by a full debrief with the art team to discuss your methodology and skills.
  • Step 3: a final interview with our CEO to confirm your arrival and finalise our collaboration.

What we offer

  • A full-time executive (cadre) position on a 35-hour week, for a real work/life balance.
  • Comprehensive health insurance, 100% paid by the studio.
  • The opportunity to join a project from its very beginning, within a close-knit, experienced team that proved itself on Empire of the Ants.
  • The quality of life of La Rochelle: a lively digital scene, culture, and the ocean right on your doorstep.
