About AARISE.

AARISE is an independent video game studio based in La Rochelle, France, founded by the original creators of Empire of the Ants. Guided by its motto, “The Art of Immersion”, the studio crafts focused, premium “double-A” games across different genres, each built around a world worth stepping into.
