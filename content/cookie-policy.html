This page explains which cookies and trackers the AARISE website may place on your device, why, and how you stay in control of your choices.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file saved by your browser when you visit a website. It can, for example, remember a preference or measure traffic. Similar technologies (local storage, pixels) are treated the same way on this page.

Your consent

On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or reject everything. You can also choose category by category with the “Manage my cookies” button below. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and no analytics or marketing cookie is placed until you agree.

Your choice is kept for 6 months, after which you will be asked again. You can change it or withdraw your consent at any time, using the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of every page or the button below. If you withdraw consent, the related cookies are deleted.

Cookies we use

Necessary cookies (always on)

These are essential for the site to work and do not require your consent.

Analytics (with your consent)

When analytics is enabled, we use Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see which pages are viewed, so we can improve the site. These cookies are only placed if you accept the “Analytics” category.

Marketing (with your consent)

These cookies measure how well our campaigns perform and show you relevant ads on other websites and social networks. No marketing cookie is used at the moment. If we add any (for example Google Ads, TikTok or Meta), they will be listed here and only placed with your consent.

YouTube videos

Game trailers on project pages are embedded using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode: no cookie is placed until you start a video. Once you play it, Google may store information on your device under its own privacy policy.

Browser settings

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may however stop the site from remembering your choices. To learn more about your rights, visit the website of the French data protection authority, the CNIL.
