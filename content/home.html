The studio

A new studio. A proven team.

AARISE was founded by the original creators of Empire of the Ants. We took players into the tactical heart of nature; now we are building what comes next.

Bringing a complex game to market taught us what we do best, and what we needed to do better. We kept the lessons, and the team. Different genres, different worlds, one obsession: making you forget you are holding a controller.
