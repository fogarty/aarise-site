About

Born from ambition. Forged by experience.

AARISE is more than a new studio: it is the next chapter of a team that has already taken a complex vision all the way to players.

Our story

From the heart of nature to new worlds.

We are the original creators of Empire of the Ants, the game that took players into the tactical heart of nature.

That project taught us a great deal about our strengths and, just as importantly, about where we needed to improve. We have embraced those lessons and turned past challenges into an advantage.

We are back sharper, more focused, and with the resilience that only comes from bringing an ambitious game to market. Today we work on several kinds of games, from colourful adventures to more mature experiences, always with the same goal: worlds that resonate.

Our name

AA + Arise.

Our name is a statement of purpose.

AA stands for the games we love and make: premium, focused craftsmanship, with heart, soul and great gameplay, without the bloat of AAA.

Arise is our promise to raise that standard, using our collective experience to bring new worlds to light.

What drives us

The art of immersion.

I.

Believable worlds

Immersion is not a genre. A cartoon island and a dark fantasy realm both need rules, coherence and soul.

II.

Gameplay that holds you

Beautiful worlds are not enough. What keeps players inside is how the game feels in their hands, minute after minute.

III.

Detail, everywhere

A sound, an animation, a line of text: immersion breaks at the weakest point. We polish all of them.
