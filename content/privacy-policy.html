This policy explains what personal data the AARISE website processes, why, for how long, and how to exercise your rights. We collect as little as possible: the site has no user accounts and no newsletter, and our forms only ask for what we need to answer you.

Data controller

AARISE SAS, 6 rue Virginie Hériot, 17000 La Rochelle, France. For any question about your data: privacy@aarise.games. AARISE SAS has not appointed a data protection officer.

The data we process

Details about cookies and how to change your choice are in our cookie policy.

Who can access your data

Your data is never sold or rented. It is accessible to the AARISE team and to our service providers, who process it only on our behalf:

  • Cloudways Ltd (Malta), website hosting, on a server operated by The Constant Company, LLC (Vultr) located in France, where form submissions are also stored;
  • Google Ireland Limited (Ireland), analytics with Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, only with your consent; and Google Workspace, our email and the tools we use to follow job applications (your application and CV are stored in Google Drive and Google Sheets);
  • Anthropic, PBC (United States), which reads the CV sent with a job application to prefill our application tracking sheet (experience, skills, tools). Anthropic acts on our behalf only and does not use this data to train its models. Every application is read and assessed by a person; no decision is made automatically.

Transfers outside the European Union

Analytics data and job application data may be transferred to the United States by Google and Anthropic. These transfers are covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and, for Google, by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google is certified. All other site data is hosted in France.

Videos and social networks

Game trailers on project pages are embedded using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode: no data is sent to Google until you start a video. Links to other websites (game websites, stores, social networks) are plain links: no data is shared with them unless you click. Once on their websites, their own privacy policies apply.

Children

This website is mainly aimed at adults and professionals (press, partners, job applicants). We do not seek to collect data about children. Children should ask a parent before answering the cookie banner (in France, children under 15 cannot consent to analytics on their own). A parent can contact us at any time to have data about their child deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, to withdraw your consent at any time, and, under French law, to set instructions regarding your data after your death.

To exercise them, write to privacy@aarise.games. We reply within one month. To withdraw your cookie consent, use the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of every page.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France (cnil.fr), or with the authority of your country of residence.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS only, and access to its administration is restricted and protected. Our service providers apply their own security measures under contract.

Changes

This policy may change, for example if we add a newsletter. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
